Duck Thieves are a punk panto party pop band, bringing fast back-and-forth lyrics, spiky rhythmical art-punk and elements of theatrical performance art and big guitar solos.

As a collective of queer, disabled, neurospiciness minorities, they write songs about standing up to racism, feminism, the British East Asian experience and personal life stories.
Live, the band are known for high energy prop-laden theatrics with dramatic interaction between the members and 5-way vocals.

The Coventry band take their name after the first ever film made in Hong Kong, ‘Stealing a Roasted Duck’, which was destroyed and consequently no one alive has seen.

Duck Thieves have played on the main stage at Godiva Festival and supported The Specials at their homecoming gig at Coventry Cathedral ruins as well as Terry Hall's Homegrown Sessions. Recently played at Bearded Theory Festival,  Greenbelt Festival and returned to Equinox Festival for a second year in a row. (2025)
They have released multiple singles and EPs to critical acclaim including Act One, Dance Like a Duck Thief, In Liverpool We’ll Barn Dance and their recent Eyes Up Here.
